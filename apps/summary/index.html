How to read these comparisons

Each grey dot represents one expert recording in the selected phase. The shaded band covers the central half of expert values, with a quarter below and a quarter above. The dark line marks the median; the blue diamond shows this recording.

These measurements describe differences from the expert recordings. They are not a skill score or a target range.

Extreme expert values are hidden from the plot but remain included in the calculations. A current value outside the displayed scale is shown with an arrow and its actual number.

Measurements cover the entire selected phase. Stroke length averages eligible complete strokes. Tool speed uses valid recorded movement and time. Removal rate and stroke frequency use the full phase duration.

Drill Visibility shows the estimated total time with poor drill visibility during the selected phase.
